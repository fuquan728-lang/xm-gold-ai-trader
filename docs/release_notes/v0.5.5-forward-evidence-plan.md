# v0.5.5 Forward Evidence Plan

This checkpoint defines the read-only evidence gates required before any future
v0.6 parameter-candidate or strategy-change research phase.

## Scope

- Extended `scripts/strategy_hypothesis_lab.py --json`.
- Added top-level `forward_evidence_plan`.
- Added pytest coverage proving the plan exists, keeps production decisions
  disabled, and keeps safety fields clean.

This is a planning checkpoint only. It does not change production strategy,
risk settings, safety gates, order routing, or AI annotation behavior.

## 1. Forward Enriched Live Dry-Run Requirement

Required before v0.6 research can begin:

- minimum enriched closed bars: `500`
- required final dry-run `SIGNAL` evidence: at least `5`
- required diagnostics coverage: at least `95%`
- required diagnostics include SMA values, previous SMA values, crossover state,
  ATR, stop distance, computed lot, normalized lot, broker volume constraints,
  spread fields, and failed rule names.

Observation remains read-only and must keep `orders_sent: 0`.

## 2. Out-of-Sample Historical Split

Required split:

- chronological train/test split
- train fraction: `70%`
- test fraction: `30%`
- no parameter selection on the test split

Reports must separate:

- candidate signal count
- final feasible signal count
- minimum-lot feasibility
- top block reasons

Train and test results must be reported separately.

## 3. Risk And Execution Feasibility

Required feasibility scenarios:

- realistic account balances: `$500`, `$1,000`, `$2,500`, `$5,000`, `$10,000`
- risk percentages: `0.25%`, `0.5%`, `1.0%`
- fixed risk budgets: `$2.50`, `$5.00`, `$10.00`, `$15.00`, `$25.00`

Broker constraints must include:

- `volume_min`
- `volume_step`
- `volume_max`
- `trade_tick_size`
- `trade_tick_value`
- `trade_stops_level`
- `max_spread_points`

The plan preserves the existing rule: never round up to broker minimum lot when
risk is too small.

## 4. Market-Condition Sensitivity

Future research must evaluate:

- spread regime sensitivity
- ATR / volatility regime sensitivity
- stop-distance distribution
- risk-per-lot distribution
- computed and normalized lot distributions

## 5. Trade-Quality Diagnostics

Future research must include, when available:

- MAE/MFE analysis
- drawdown proxy
- max consecutive losses proxy
- BUY/SELL balance
- raw candidate spam penalty
- feasible signal quality summary

High raw candidate count is not sufficient evidence of strategy quality.

## 6. Decision Gates

v0.6 research may begin only after:

- at least `500` enriched closed-bar observations
- at least `5` final dry-run `SIGNAL` observations
- at least `95%` diagnostics coverage
- out-of-sample split completed
- minimum-lot feasibility reported under realistic assumptions
- spread and ATR regime sensitivity reported

Production strategy changes remain blocked by:

- zero or insufficient final live dry-run `SIGNAL` evidence
- missing enriched diagnostics coverage
- no out-of-sample validation
- unresolved minimum-lot feasibility dependence on unrealistic risk budgets
- unmeasured drawdown, MAE, or MFE behavior
- any safety boundary violation

Explicit decision:

- `production_ready: false`
- `production_strategy_change_recommended: false`
- `live_order_enablement_recommended: false`
- no live order recommendation

## Safety Guarantees

- `orders_sent: 0`
- `order_check_called: false`
- `order_send_called: false`
- Default `configs/xm_gold_ai_trader.demo.yaml` keeps
  `execution.allow_order_send: false`.
- No AI trading.
- AI annotation remains read-only commentary.
- No martingale, no grid, and no automatic lot increase after loss.
