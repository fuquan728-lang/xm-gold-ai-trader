# v0.5.1 Minimum-Lot Feasibility Study

This checkpoint extends the read-only offline strategy hypothesis lab with a
minimum-lot feasibility study for `LOT_BELOW_VOLUME_MIN`.

## Scope

- Extended `scripts/strategy_hypothesis_lab.py --json`.
- Added per-hypothesis `minimum_lot_feasibility` diagnostics.
- Added top-level `minimum_lot_feasibility_study` for the current baseline.
- Added pytest coverage for blocked and feasible hypothetical risk budgets.

The study uses only historical/cached bars, stored `GOLD_` symbol metadata, and
the existing `RiskManager` calculations. It does not connect to MT5, does not
call `order_check`, and does not call `order_send`.

## New Diagnostics

For historical candidates, the lab now reports:

- computed lot distribution
- normalized lot distribution
- risk-per-lot distribution
- stop-distance and ATR distributions
- broker `volume_min`, `volume_step`, and `volume_max`
- risk shortfall to broker minimum lot
- minimum account risk amount required to trade `volume_min`
- account balance required at the current risk percentage
- risk percentage required at the current account-equity assumption
- hypothetical risk-budget scenarios and candidate feasibility counts

All scenario outputs are labeled `hypothetical_only: true` and
`not_production_settings: true`.

  - min: about `0.52%`

Hypothetical risk-budget scenarios:

- current `$2.50` risk amount: `0 / 157` candidates feasible
- `$5.00` risk amount: `0 / 157` candidates feasible
- `$12.50` risk amount: `53 / 157` candidates feasible
- `$25.00` risk amount: `131 / 157` candidates feasible
- all candidates would require up to about `$82.85` risk amount for minimum lot

These are research diagnostics only. They are not recommendations and do not
modify production risk settings.
