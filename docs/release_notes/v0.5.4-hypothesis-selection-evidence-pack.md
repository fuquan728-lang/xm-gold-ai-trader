# v0.5.4 Hypothesis Selection Evidence Pack

This checkpoint adds a read-only evidence pack to the offline strategy
hypothesis lab.

## Scope

- Extended `scripts/strategy_hypothesis_lab.py --json`.
- Added top-level `hypothesis_selection_evidence_pack`.
- Added pytest coverage for evidence-pack presence, deterministic selected
  hypothesis, production-disabled decision fields, and clean safety fields.

The evidence pack summarizes the research path from v0.5.0 through v0.5.3. It
does not connect to MT5, does not call `order_check`, does not call
`order_send`, and does not create a production recommendation to trade.

## Evidence Summarized

The evidence pack includes:

- baseline candidate feasibility
- minimum-lot feasibility bottleneck
- fixed risk-budget frontier
- normalized hypothesis ranking
- top ranked hypothesis by risk budget
- why `current_baseline_risk_gated` remains preferred offline
- why raw candidate spam is penalized
- why `sma_10_30_risk_gated` is not selected yet despite being close at `$25`

## Current Selection

Selected offline hypothesis:

- `current_baseline_risk_gated`

It remains preferred because it is risk-gated, has balanced historical BUY/SELL
candidates, avoids the candidate-spam penalty, and ranked first across the
tested fixed risk budgets.

At the `$25` hypothetical risk budget:

- `current_baseline_risk_gated`: score about `0.907772`, `131 / 157` feasible
- `sma_10_30_risk_gated`: score about `0.906194`, `179 / 201` feasible

The `sma_10_30_risk_gated` hypothesis is not selected yet because it expands raw
candidate count, is close only under a hypothetical risk budget, and lacks
longer enriched live-sample and out-of-sample evidence.
