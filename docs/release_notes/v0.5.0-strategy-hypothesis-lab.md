# v0.5.0 Strategy Hypothesis Lab

This checkpoint adds a read-only offline research lab for explaining why the
current baseline produces zero final actionable live dry-run signals.

## Scope

- Added `scripts/strategy_hypothesis_lab.py --json`.
- The script evaluates offline hypotheses against collected `GOLD_` M15 bars
  and stored symbol metadata.
- It does not connect to MT5, does not call `order_check`, and does not call
  `order_send`.
- All reported results are hypothetical research diagnostics and are not used
  for trading.

## Hypotheses

The lab compares:

- current selected SMA crossover with normal risk feasibility gates
- current selected SMA crossover in candidate-only diagnostic mode
- alternate SMA fast/slow pairs
- trend-continuation diagnostic mode without immediate crossover requirement
- relaxed SMA-slope candidate-only diagnostic mode

Production strategy defaults, live dry-run signal logic, safety gates, order
routing, and AI annotation behavior are unchanged.

## Current Findings

Using the current collected `data/gold_m15.csv` sample and offline risk
assumptions:

- The current risk-gated baseline generated historical candidates but zero
  final theoretical signals after feasibility gates.
- Candidate-only diagnostics show that crossover candidates exist historically.
- Risk-gated variants remained blocked by estimated broker minimum-lot
  feasibility at the configured small-risk assumptions.
- Trend-continuation and relaxed candidate-only diagnostics produce many more
  hypothetical candidates, but they are research-only and not production logic.

These findings point to two separate research questions:

- Candidate scarcity in live closed-bar samples remains dominated by
  `NO_ACTIONABLE_SIGNAL`.
- Candidate feasibility at the current small-risk assumptions is dominated by
  `LOT_BELOW_VOLUME_MIN`.

## Safety Guarantees

- `orders_sent: 0`
- `order_check_called: false`
- `order_send_called: false`

  `execution.allow_order_send: false`.
