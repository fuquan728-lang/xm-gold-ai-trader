# v0.5.2 Risk-Budget Scenario Frontier

This checkpoint extends the read-only offline strategy hypothesis lab with a
risk-budget frontier for `GOLD_` minimum-lot feasibility.

## Scope

- Extended `scripts/strategy_hypothesis_lab.py --json`.
- Added `risk_budget_frontier` under each hypothesis
  `minimum_lot_feasibility` block.
- The top-level current-baseline `minimum_lot_feasibility_study` now includes
  the same frontier report.
- Added pytest coverage for account-balance/risk-percentage and fixed-dollar
  risk-budget scenarios.

The frontier uses only historical/cached candidates and stored broker symbol
metadata. It does not connect to MT5, does not call `order_check`, and does not
call `order_send`.

## Scenario Sets

Account-balance/risk-percentage matrix:

- account balances: `$500`, `$1,000`, `$2,500`, `$5,000`, `$6,000`, `$10,000`
- risk percentages: `0.25%`, `0.5%`, `1.0%`

Fixed risk budgets:

- `$2.50`, `$5.00`, `$10.00`, `$15.00`, `$25.00`

Each scenario reports:

- feasible candidate count
- infeasible candidate count
- feasible percentage
- median computed lot
- median normalized lot
- median risk shortfall
- minimum required balance estimate

All rows are labeled `hypothetical_only: true` and
`not_production_settings: true`.

## Current Baseline Frontier Findings

For the current risk-gated baseline over `157` historical crossover candidates:

- `$1,000` balance at `0.25%` risk (`$2.50`) makes `0 / 157` candidates
  feasible.
- `$2,500` balance at `0.5%` risk (`$12.50`) makes `53 / 157` candidates
  feasible.
- `$6,000` balance at `0.25%` risk (`$15.00`) makes `80 / 157` candidates
  feasible.
- `$10,000` balance at `1.0%` risk (`$100.00`) makes `157 / 157` candidates
  feasible.

Fixed-dollar risk-budget scenarios:

- `$2.50`: `0 / 157` feasible
- `$5.00`: `0 / 157` feasible
- `$10.00`: `21 / 157` feasible
- `$15.00`: `80 / 157` feasible
- `$25.00`: `131 / 157` feasible

These are offline feasibility diagnostics only. They are not recommendations
and do not modify production risk settings.
